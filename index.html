Simulation

Deals real 8-deck shoes and tests the pattern methods, to show how often they really win.

Every hand is dealt from a shuffled shoe. The card math is exact, but the house edge stays on almost every hand. A "strong lean" is only the model's best estimate. No hand is ever sure. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
